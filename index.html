A Day with the Bookshop Cat

A story told one scroll at a time.

Scroll down ↓

9:05 AM

Morning

The shop opens at nine. Kitty opens at 9:05.

Every morning the shutters rattle up over the little bookstall on the corner, and every morning Kitty strolls in five minutes late, as if the shop had been waiting for him. Which, of course, it had.

He inspects the new arrivals the way a critic reviews a novel: slowly, and mostly by sitting on them. Today's stack of puzzle books gets the verdict — sunny, firm, and perfect for watching customers.

2:30 PM

Afternoon

Two cats, one lap, zero interest in the plot.

By afternoon the sun has moved to the park across the street, so Kitty moves with it. His friend Marmalade is already there, curled up on the lap of a regular who comes by every day with a paperback and a soft blanket.

The reader tries to turn a page. Marmalade leans on it. Kitty climbs aboard and pushes his head into their hand until reading turns into petting.

7:45 PM

Evening

Some stories are best read with your eyes closed.

When the streetlights flicker on, Kitty heads home to a quiet room and finds an open book someone left on the bed. He lies down beside it, just to see how the story ends.

His eyelids grow heavier with every line. The book will still be there in the morning. So will he.

The End

Goodnight, Kitty.

Author: Zekai Wan
